Before we begin

A clear first step.
A defined commitment.

What happens after you contact Collatio Labs?

We discuss your objective, the process or program involved, and the information needed to assess fit. Where there is a suitable engagement, the next step is a proposal defining scope, deliverables, responsibilities, and price.

What does the one-week review require from us?

A defined process, an accountable owner, access to relevant source material, and input from the people doing the work. We agree participation and access before the review begins.

What will we have at the end?

A documented view of the process today, prioritized improvements, and a costed plan for the first phase. The implementation sequence and acceptance criteria help you decide what to fund and how to measure progress.

Who is accountable for the engagement?

Accountability, delivery responsibilities, and decision authority are agreed in the scope, so ownership remains clear throughout the work.
