How we work

We run our own public-sector work under the controls we recommend.

Our business-development platform screens and qualifies federal, state and local opportunities and drafts our responses.

The controls

Our responses now pass through five controls:

  1. 01Validated claim by claim against a single system of record.
  2. 02Screened by an automated compliance gate.
  3. 03Cross-checked by AI models from other vendors.
  4. 04Sent by a person. The platform drafts but cannot send.
  5. 05Recorded in an audit trail.

Demonstrators

Selected proposals include working software.

Each demonstrator is built to the solicitation’s stated requirements, on public or synthetic data.

Legislative Tracking System

Built for the Washington State Department of Revenue’s solicitation, on public legislative data; not deployed. It gives agency executives one view of each bill: status, analysis stage, analysis due date and the deadlines before its committee hearing, each deadline marked with its source. It also compares versions of a bill and underlines the text a later version adds.

  • Uses the Legislature’s public record of 3,762 bills, 31,534 actions and 21,947 documents; agency tracking data is synthetic.
  • Zero violations on automated WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility checks.

Services

Redesign the process. Then automate what remains.

Automation layered onto an unchanged process seldom lowers its cost. We start with the process, not a tool, and measure the result by operating cost, cycle time, capacity and control.

01

AI operating-model assessment

We identify where AI can reduce cost and manual effort, what governance it requires and in what order to introduce it. Leadership receives a sequenced, costed plan it can fund and track.

02

Process redesign and intelligent automation

We map how the work runs, remove steps that add cost without adding control and automate the rest. Standard cases then run without manual handling, exceptions go to a person, and consequential decisions wait for human approval.

03

Transformation office and program recovery

We set up the governance that keeps a transformation on course: one plan, clear decision rights, risk management and executive reporting. When a program has stalled, we stabilize and re-plan it.

04

Legacy modernization

We modernize the systems the work still depends on and build new ones where no existing system fits. We retire systems kept running only for look-ups, audits and records requests, and keep their records and reports accessible.

05

Interim transformation leadership

When a transformation or program needs an accountable leader before a permanent appointment, we lead it in the interim and hand over a stable program.

Custodia

Retire the system. Keep the record verifiable.

Custodia is a legacy-system retirement solution that preserves records and reports in tamper-evident, independently verifiable archives, reconciles them to the source system and identifies reconciliation gaps for audit.

Status: built and tested, not deployed.

AI use. Client data is never used to train models and is never sent to the cloud AI services used in our internal operations.
